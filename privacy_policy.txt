PRIVACY POLICY
Sowda App (Daşoguz Söwda)

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Sowda mobile application (the “App”, “we”, “us”) collects and processes, how it is used, and what rights you have.

1. GENERAL INFORMATION
Sowda is a classifieds service for the Dashoguz region. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT
2.1. Data you provide:
- phone number (for registration, sign-in, and SMS/OTP verification);
- profile data (name, profile photo, and other information you enter);
- listing and store content (text, photos, videos, prices, categories, contact details in a listing);
- messages and requests related to use of the service.

2.2. Data collected automatically:
- device identifiers and technical information needed for the App to work;
- authentication data (session token) stored locally on the device;
- location data (if you grant location permission) — for map features and related functions;
- push notification data (Firebase Cloud Messaging token) — to deliver notifications;
- error logs and technical logs needed for service stability.

2.3. Data from device permissions (with your consent):
- internet access;
- location access (precise / approximate);
- notification access;
- camera and gallery access — when adding photos/videos to listings or your profile.

You may revoke permissions in your device settings; some App features may then become unavailable.

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING
We use data to:
- create and manage your account;
- publish and display listings and stores;
- enable contact between users within the service (for example, calling a number from a listing);
- send service and informational push notifications;
- show location and operate map features;
- maintain security and prevent fraud and abuse;
- improve the App and provide user support;
- comply with applicable law.

4. SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES
We do not sell your personal data.

Data may be shared with:
- infrastructure and service providers needed to run the App (hosting, API, SMS/OTP delivery, push notifications via Firebase / Google);
- competent authorities when required by law;
- other parties only with your consent or when necessary to provide a feature you requested.

Public information in listings (description, photos, phone number you choose to show, etc.) may be visible to other users of the App.

5. DATA RETENTION
We retain data for as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy, or for periods required by law.
Settings and an authentication token may be stored locally on the device (for example, via SharedPreferences). You may sign out or uninstall the App to stop local storage.

6. DATA SECURITY
We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. YOUR RIGHTS
You may:
- request access to your personal data;
- correct inaccurate profile data;
- request deletion of your account and related data (subject to lawful retention grounds);
- withdraw consents where applicable (for example, disable location or notifications);
- limit use of certain App features.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. CHILDREN’S DATA
The App is not intended for persons under 16. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal data. If you become aware of such a case, contact us and we will take steps to delete the relevant data.

9. THIRD-PARTY LINKS AND SERVICES
The App may contain links to or integrations with third-party services. We do not control their privacy practices and are not responsible for how they process data. Please review their policies separately.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Policy. The current version will be available in the App and/or on a related resource. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy, unless applicable law requires otherwise.
